Everything you need to know about fixed mountain cab fares, route travel permits, weather advisories, and booking with Fab Cab.

The fixed taxi fare from Bagdogra Airport (IXB) or NJP Railway Station to Gangtok starts from ₹3,400 to ₹3,500 for a comfortable Toyota Innova Crysta or Mahindra Scorpio. All quotes are transparent and fixed, inclusive of tolls, state border road tax, fuel, and driver charges with zero hidden surge pricing.

As an officially registered operator with the Government of Sikkim, Fab Cab takes care of all required route travel permissions for you. You only need to share digital copies of your Voter ID or Passport and passport photos when booking. Our Gangtok team handles all official paperwork so your journey is relaxed and hassle-free.

For terrain clearance and comfort, we recommend Toyota Innova Crysta, Mahindra Scorpio 4x4, and Bolero Camper 4WD. All our high-altitude fleet vehicles carry verified all-weather snow chains and are driven by locally certified mountain chauffeurs with emergency safety training.
