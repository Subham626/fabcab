Weather Advisories

Alternative scenic routes if high passes are closed.

1. Booking Confirmation & Fixed Fares

All tour packages and car rental reservations confirmed via Fab Cab feature fixed, transparent rates including mountain permit assistance, fuel, driver allowances, and standard toll charges. No on-spot mountain surge pricing is levied once booked.

2. Mountain Road Conditions & Weather Advisories

High-altitude routes in Sikkim (Nathula Pass, Zero Point, Gurudongmar Lake) are subject to sudden snowfall, landslides, and Indian Army clearance advisories. In the event of road closures by authorities, our Gangtok support team will arrange safe alternative routes or partial credits in accordance with government protocols.

3. Route Travel Permissions & ID Requirements

Guests must provide valid government-issued IDs along with passport-size photographs at least 24-48 hours before the scheduled travel date. Our Gangtok team handles all official paperwork with tourism authorities smoothly.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations made 7+ days prior to the journey start date receive a full refund minus nominal permit processing fees. Emergency cancellations due to official road closures are credited towards rescheduled trips.
